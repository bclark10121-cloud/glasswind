# Glasswind 0.2.0: Climb and glide

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.1.x. Your save stays and your bike stays built.

## What is new

- Climbing. Push into any steep rock, the Leaning Tower or the Buried Ship's hull to climb it. Up and down climb, left and right go around.
- Stamina. Climbing tires you (a gentle limit). Rest on the tower's two ledges or on flat ground. Run out and you slide down slowly. Settings > No climbing limit turns it off.
- The glide bubble. Fall from anywhere high and a bubble opens. Steer it down. Jump while climbing to let go.
- A new start. The wind took your bike apart. Find its three parts around Home Camp to ride.
- People. Elder Suna at Home Camp. Taro, Mira, Ilo and Noor at the Nomad Camp.
- The first two requests. Carry water from Taro to Elder Suna for a Courier token. Climb the Leaning Tower and wake the beacon for Mira for a Scout token.
- New achievements to earn: Summit and Soft Landing (glide for ten seconds).

## Test checklist

1. Start a new game. Find the three glowing parts and ride.
2. Talk to Mira and Taro at the Nomad Camp (E, X or Act).
3. Climb the Leaning Tower. Rest on a ledge. Wake the beacon at the top.
4. Walk off the top and glide down. Steer the bubble.
5. Take the water to Elder Suna. Tell Mira about the beacon.
6. Try a steep rock face and the Dry Falls cliff.
7. Try Settings > No climbing limit.
