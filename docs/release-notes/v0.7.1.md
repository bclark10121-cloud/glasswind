# Saffron Drift 0.7.1: New name

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.7.0. Your save stays.

## Five minute test

1. Open the new page: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/saffron-drift/
2. Install the APK over 0.7.0. The start screen says Saffron Drift.
3. Tap Continue. Your 0.7.0 save loads with your coins, scrap, dyes and upgrades.
4. Open Settings. Your old settings are still there.
5. Open the Game Shelf. The card says Saffron Drift and links to the new page.
