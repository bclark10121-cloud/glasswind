# Glasswind 0.5.1: One panel at a time

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.5.0. Your save stays.

## Fixed from your phone test

- The Menu stayed on top of Settings. Now every Menu row opens in place of the Menu: Settings, How to play, Map, Journal and Records. Back brings the Menu back, and the game stays paused.

## Also

- New games made from the starter now ship with the sound bus file, so their music and sound work in the browser from day one. They also get the Menu fix.

## Test checklist

1. Open the Menu and tap Settings. Only Settings shows.
2. Tap Back. The Menu comes back.
3. Do the same for How to play, Map, Journal and Records.
4. Tap Resume. Play goes on.
