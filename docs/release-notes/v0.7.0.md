# Glasswind 0.7.0: The magic carpet

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.6.1. Your save stays.

## What is new

- The hover bike is now a magic carpet. It is our own design, not any film's: a woven terracotta rug with a sand border, dark brown zigzags, a diamond in the middle and tassels at both ends (your pick, Terracotta desert).
- The carpet ripples as it flies, faster the faster you go, and tilts with the dunes.
- The rider stands on it surf style and leans into turns (your pick).
- Rana's paints are now carpet dyes: Sunset coral, Night indigo, Oasis green and Desert gold. Boost smoke is now a boost trail in the same colors as before.
- Kip's upgrades have carpet names: Wind weave (top speed), Gust charm (boost), Lift thread (hops) and the Gliding stone.
- The three pieces at Home Camp are now the woven rug, the tassel cord and the wind thread, and they finish an empty loom.
- Every button, hint and line of talk says carpet. The Bike button is now Carpet.
- The start screen shows the rider standing on a rippling carpet.

## Fixed

- The black dot at the start of the boost trail. Godot's built-in particle emitter drew it. The trail is now a set of soft puffs the game moves itself, so the dot can't come back.

## Test checklist

1. Start a new game. Find the three pieces and step onto the carpet.
2. Ride fast and watch the carpet ripple. Turn hard and watch the rider lean.
3. Boost. You should see a soft trail and no black dot.
4. Buy a dye from Rana and a trail color. Check both.
5. Upgrade something with Kip and check the new names.

## Judgment calls

- The rider uses the standing idle motion. A cross-legged sit or a crouch on boost would need a new Meshy motion. It's in the backlog.
- Your save keeps your dye and trail choices. The old paint you bought becomes the matching dye.
- The game's code still calls the carpet "bike" inside. Only the player sees "carpet".
