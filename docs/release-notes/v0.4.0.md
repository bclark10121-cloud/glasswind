# Glasswind 0.4.0: Easier climbs

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.3.0. Your save stays.

Ideas from the Sable review in the backlog: 9, 10, 11, 12, 15 and 19.

## What is new

- Climbs start on purpose. Push into a wall for a moment, or jump at it. Brushing past a rock no longer grabs it or spends stamina.
- Holding still on a wall is free. Stop and look around to plan your route. Only moving tires you.
- Jump at a wall and you grab it higher up, for free.
- The camera pulls back and up while you climb, so you see the wall above you.
- In a talk, the camera turns side on and frames you and the person you talk to. It steps round tents and walls.
- Camera behind you: R on the keyboard, the right stick click on a controller. You can change it in Settings > Change controls.
- The Journal in the Menu lists each open request, who asked and where to go next.
- Updrafts. Rising sand marks warm air by the Dry Falls cliff, the Leaning Tower, the Buried Ship and in the open dunes. Glide into it and the bubble climbs again.

## Test checklist

1. Walk past the Leaning Tower without pushing in. You should not grab on.
2. Push into the tower for a moment. You grab on. Jump at it instead and you grab on higher.
3. Hold still on the wall. The stamina bar should not drop.
4. Check the camera while climbing: can you see the wall above you?
5. Talk to Elder Suna. You should both be in the picture.
6. Press R or click the right stick. The camera swings behind you.
7. Open the Menu > Journal with a request open. Check it says where to go.
8. Glide off the Leaning Tower into the rising sand at its foot. The bubble should climb.

## Judgment calls

- The push to start a climb is 0.35 seconds. Shorter grabs by accident, longer feels slow.
- Play pauses during a talk, so the camera cuts to the talk view instead of sweeping round.
- The updrafts carry you up to 70 meters over the sand.
