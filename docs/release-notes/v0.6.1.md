# Glasswind 0.6.1: Coins and looks

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.6.0. Your save stays.

## Fixed from your phone test

- No boost smoke on the phone. The real problem was bigger: on a phone only your first finger acts like a mouse. While your thumb held the stick, a second finger on Boost, Jump, Bike or Act never reached the button. All four buttons now read every finger, so boost works while you steer and the smoke shows.
- The smoke now starts clear of the bike's tail, without the dark spot.

## What is new

- Gold coins. 95 coins sit in 12 trails: down the road from Home Camp, along the Old Road arches, up the Dry Falls ramp, round the Oasis, round a sunken statue, along the Buried Ship's deck and in an arc off the Leaning Tower that only a glide reaches.
- Rana the trader at the Nomad Camp. Talk once to meet Rana, then again to open the stall.
  - Bike paint: Sunset coral, Night indigo and Oasis green for 10 coins each, Desert gold for 15.
  - Boost smoke: Coral, Teal and Violet for 12 coins each.
  - Looks you own switch back and forth for free.
- A coin counter beside scrap at the top left. Coin totals on the Map and the journey postcard.
- Scrap stays for Kip's upgrades.

## Test checklist

1. On the phone, steer with your thumb and press Boost with another finger. You should see smoke and feel the speed.
2. Try Jump and Bike with a second finger too.
3. Ride down the road from Home Camp through the coins.
4. Talk to Rana twice and buy a paint and a smoke color. Check the bike and the smoke.
5. Glide off the Leaning Tower toward the east to catch the coins in the air.
