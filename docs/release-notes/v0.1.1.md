# Glasswind 0.1.1: The rider

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.1.0. Your save stays.

## What is new

- The rider is the real character now: a young desert nomad in a teal head wrap, amber goggles, a long gold scarf and a coral wrap. Made for Glasswind through Gemini and Meshy.
- On foot the rider stands, walks and runs with your speed, and jumps.
- On the bike the rider sits on the deck.

## Still to come

- The Meshy hover bike (the bike is still boxes).
- Climbing and gliding in 0.2. The climb motion is ready.

## Test checklist

1. Start or continue a game. Walk around Home Camp. Check the walk turns into a run as you push the stick further.
2. Jump on foot.
3. Get on the bike. Check the rider sits on it and does not poke through it, at speed and in turns.
4. Jump off the Dry Falls ramp and watch the rider in the air.
5. Note how smooth it runs on your phone with the new rider.
