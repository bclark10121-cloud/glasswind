# Saffron Drift v0.7.3

Fixes from your playtest sweep on 2026-11-19. Climbing no longer puts the rider through the wall. The boost hits harder and leaves a trail you can see. The camera stays calm in turns. The game now runs on Godot 4.7.2.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.2. Your saves stay.
2. Ride the carpet at speed and hold Boost. You feel a kick, hear a whoosh and see two pale trails behind the carpet. The view widens a little.
3. Steer hard left or right at speed. The camera eases round behind you instead of swinging with the carpet.
4. Climb the Buried Ship's side, then go round its end. The rider stays on the outside of the hull the whole way.
5. Climb the Leaning Tower. Under each ledge you pull up onto it instead of passing through it. At the top you step onto the roof.

## Fixed

- Climbing went through walls (BC, 2026-11-19). The game treated the tower, the ship's hull, the giant statue and the pillar as round, but they are boxes. Climbers now follow each wall's real shape, round its corners, and hang a little off the face.
- The tower's ledges and roof sat off to one side of the tilted tower. They now follow its lean.
- Climbing up under a ledge or the roof now pulls you up onto it, so your head never goes through the slab.
- The ship's deck now slopes with the hull, so you stand on it, not inside it.

## Changed

- Boost (BC: felt weak, smoke hard to see): an instant speed kick, a whoosh, a short rumble, faster speed-up and the view widens a little while you boost (not with Reduce motion on). The trail comes from both back corners, with more, bigger and brighter puffs that last longer.
- Camera in turns (BC: swings too much): the camera now eases round behind the carpet at a set pace. In a long hard turn it trails a little, then settles behind you within about a second once you straighten out.
- Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19). No change you should notice.

## Shared kit

Version 0.7.3 installs over 0.7.2 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Boost feels strong and the trail is easy to see.
- [ ] Hard turns: the camera feels calm, not floaty.
- [ ] Climb the ship's side and its end. No part of the rider inside the hull.
- [ ] Climb the tower to the top. You pull up onto both ledges and the roof.
- [ ] Climb the statue and the pillar.
- [ ] Frame rate on your phone with the bigger trail.

## Judgment calls

1. Climbing up under a tower ledge pulls you up onto it. Before, you climbed through it. Push into the wall again to keep climbing.
2. The trail is brighter by default (Sand trail). The Coral, Teal and Violet trails keep their colors.
3. The camera swing limit is 1.0 radian a second (about a quarter turn in 1.5 seconds). Easy to change if it feels too slow.
4. The view widens to 80 degrees while boosting. Reduce motion turns this off.
5. The tower's roof and ledges moved by about 1 to 3 meters to sit on the tower properly. A star stone on the first ledge moved with it.
