# Glasswind 0.5.0: Sound and fires

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.4.0. Your save stays.

## Fixed from your browser test

- No music or sound in the browser. The game made its sound channels while it ran, and browsers keep those silent. The channels now ship with the game, the same fix Tiny Tailwind needed on 11-16. The title track plays on the start screen after your first press, then in play.
- Can't jump while running. Running down a slope lifted your feet off the sand for a moment, and the jump waited for the ground. Your feet now stay on the sand going downhill. A jump also works for a moment after you leave an edge, and a jump pressed just before landing happens when you land.
- Boost smoke. Hold boost and a trail of sand-colored smoke streams out of the back of the bike.

## What is new

- The Old Fires. Ilo at the Nomad Camp asks you to light three old fires on the Dry Falls lip. Climb up and walk to each one. That makes seven requests.
- Star stones make climbing last longer, a tenth more for each stone.
- Your journey so far: the Journal in the Menu now ends with a postcard of places found, people helped, stones, distance, your longest glide and your biggest jump.
- Rumble on a controller or phone: big landings, climbing over the top, lighting a fire and finding a stone. Settings > Rumble turns it off.

## Test checklist

1. In the browser, listen on the start screen after your first press. Then start a game and listen for music, wind and the bike hum.
2. Run down a dune or the Dry Falls ramp and press jump. You should jump every time.
3. Ride and hold boost. Smoke streams out of the back and stops when you let go.
4. Finish Ilo's seeds request, then talk to Ilo again for the Old Fires. Light all three and tell Ilo.
5. Find a star stone, then climb. The bar should drain a little slower.
6. Open Menu > Journal > Your journey so far.

## Judgment calls

- The jump grace is 0.2 seconds and the early press counts for 0.15 seconds.
- The Old Fires are Ilo's second request, after the seeds. They give a Scout token.
- The fires are flat-color cones on stone bowls for now, like the rest of the gray-box world.
- I could not hear the browser build from here. The fix matches the cause Tiny Tailwind had, and the tests show the music playing on the right channel. Please confirm with your ears.
