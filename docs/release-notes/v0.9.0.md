# Saffron Drift v0.9.0

Warm air works again, and the game now runs on the shared kit's explore, shop and map blocks. Most of this release is under the hood. One fix you can feel: a glide over an updraft rises again.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.2. Your saves stay: scrap, coins, upgrades, paints, smoke, places found and tassel charms.
2. Climb the Dry Falls ramp and glide out over the falls. Hold still over the warm air. The bubble should rise, not sink.
3. Visit Kip at Home Camp and buy an upgrade. Visit Rana at the Nomad Camp and buy or switch a look.
4. Ride to a place you have not found yet. Open the Map. The top line now says how much of the basin you have mapped.
5. Climb a wall until stamina runs out. You should slide down gently, the same as 0.8.2.

## Fixed

- Warm air (backlog 19). Since 0.4.0 the updrafts lifted the glide for only a moment, then gravity won and the bubble sank. A test showed a glide 20 m over a draft fell to the sand in 3 seconds. Now it rises to about 30 m in the same 3 seconds and keeps rising, up to 70 m over the sand.

## Changed

- The Map counts how much of the basin you have seen ("Mapped 12% of the basin"). The map fills in as you move and saves with your slot.
- Full screen by default in the browser (G5.11): the first tap, click or key press goes full screen. Turn it off in Settings and it stays off.
- Up to 120 frames a second on fast phones (G6.20). No 60 cap.

## Under the hood

- Walking, climbing stamina, pushing into a wall and gliding run on the kit's explore block (K6).
- Scrap, coins, Kip's upgrades and Rana's looks run on the kit's collect block (K7).
- Places found and the revealed map run on the kit's map block (K9).
- The slot still writes the 0.8 fields, so an older build reads a 0.9 save.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.22.0 (was 1.15.0). Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Version 0.9.0 installs over 0.8.2 and keeps your saves.

## Judgment calls

1. The save format did not change. The kit blocks read the old fields on load and the game writes them back. Only the revealed map is new in the slot.
2. The Map shows a "Mapped N%" line. It does not yet shade unseen parts of the map. That can come later if you want it.
3. Warm air now lifts toward 4 m a second, the speed set in 0.4.0. The cap at 70 m over the sand stays.
