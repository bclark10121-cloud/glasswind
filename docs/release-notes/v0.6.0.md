# Glasswind 0.6.0: Closer, fuller

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.5.1. Your save stays and moves with the smaller basin.

Your ask: a smaller world with more things to do, plus collecting and upgrading.

## What is new

- A smaller basin. It is now about 800 m across, 55 percent of the old size. Every place is closer, and the rim is nearer all around.
- Eight small ruins between the big places: broken walls, two half-sunk statues, a ring of wind vanes, two tall pillars and an empty camp.
- Scrap. Each ruin has a cache of 5 scrap on top, often a short climb up. 34 more single pieces lie on the dunes. That makes 42 to find. Every finished request also gives 3 scrap.
- Kip the tinker at Home Camp, by the bike stand. Talk to Kip once to meet, then again to open the workbench.
- Four upgrades with three levels each, costing 3, 6 and 9 scrap:
  - Engine: higher top speed.
  - Boost tank: boost lasts longer.
  - Hover springs: higher bike jumps.
  - Gliding stone: glide longer and faster.
- A scrap counter at the top left, and scrap totals on the Map and the journey postcard.

## Test checklist

1. Load your old save. You should be in the same spot relative to the places, just closer.
2. Ride from Home Camp to each place. Does it feel closer but not cramped?
3. Find a ruin. Climb it and grab the scrap cache on top.
4. Ride over some loose scrap. The counter goes up.
5. Talk to Kip twice and buy an upgrade. Feel the difference on the bike.
6. Check the Map and Menu > Journal > Your journey so far for the scrap totals.

## Judgment calls

- 55 percent keeps the big landmarks the same size and moves them closer. The cliffs, tower and ship did not shrink.
- Every upgrade at full level costs 72 scrap. The world holds 74 (40 in caches, 34 loose) and requests give 21 more, so you can max everything without finding every piece.
- Kip's first talk is an introduction. The workbench opens from the second talk on.
- The ruins are gray-box shapes like the rest of the world for now.
