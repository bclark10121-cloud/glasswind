# Glasswind 0.1.0: Gray box

2026-11-18. The first test build. The shapes are simple boxes while the riding, walking and camera get tuned. Kit 1.13.0.

## What is new

- One desert basin, about a mile across, ringed by hills.
- Five places to find: the Leaning Tower, the Buried Ship, the Dry Falls, the Oasis and the Nomad Camp. Home Camp is where you start.
- A hover bike that floats over the sand, drifts in turns, boosts and jumps. It leaves the ground over crests.
- A long ramp up the south side of the Dry Falls. Ride off the top for a big jump.
- Walking anywhere. Call the bike back from anywhere with one button.
- A camera behind the bike and over your shoulder on foot. It never dips into the sand or behind a dune.
- Keyboard, Xbox controller and touch. Settings > Auto accelerate lets you ride with one thumb.
- The start screen: a dune ridge at dusk, the rider crossing it, sand blowing off the crests and the stars coming out.
- Settings, five save slots, the Menu, How to play, Records with ten achievements and Change controls.

## Not in this build yet

- Climbing, gliding, camp requests, the map, day and night, real art and music. They come in 0.2 and 0.3.

## Test checklist

1. Start a new game. Get on the bike (Q, B or the Bike button).
2. Ride to all five places. Note any spot where the camera gets in the way.
3. Ride up the Dry Falls ramp and jump off the top. Big Air should unlock.
4. Get off, walk around and call the bike from far away.
5. Try the controller and touch both ways round (landscape and upright).
6. Note how smooth it runs on your phone. Settings > Picture can go lower.
