# Saffron Drift v0.8.2

Tassel charms, the last piece of The Drift outline. The six hidden star stones are now tassel charms. Each one you find hangs a gold tassel on your carpet's fringe.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.1. Your saves stay. Charms you already found show as gold tassels at once.
2. Find a tassel charm (one sits on the Leaning Tower's first ledge). A tassel on the carpet turns gold and glows a little.
3. Open the Map or the postcard. They count tassel charms.

## Changed

- Star stones are tassel charms: gold in the world, a gold tassel on the carpet for each one found. They still make climbing last a little longer.
- The words "star stone" are gone from the game, so the Star pattern piece is the only star.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.15.0, unchanged. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Version 0.8.2 installs over 0.8.1 and keeps your saves.

## Judgment calls

1. The charms keep their places and their climbing bonus. Only the name, color and the carpet tassel are new.
2. Six of the carpet's 18 tassels can turn gold, spread along both ends.
