# Saffron Drift v0.8.0

The Drift. You are Tavi, sixteen, and every child of the Hearth Caravan takes the Drift at sixteen. You ride out on grandmother's old carpet, faded to the color of sand. Five keepers each weave one pattern piece into it. Bring it home with all five and make the pattern your own.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.3. Your saves stay.
2. Start a new game in an empty slot. Gather the three carpet pieces at Home Camp. The carpet is pale sand colored.
3. Talk to Elder Suna. She sends you off on the Drift and names the five keepers.
4. Open the Journal in the Menu. It lists the five pieces still to earn and how to earn each.
5. Ride to Rana at the Nomad Camp market and buy any dye or trail color. Talk to her again. She weaves the Sun into your carpet. A small mark shows on the carpet and its colors come back a little.

## New in this version

- The Drift story (BC, 2026-11-18): the rider is Tavi (BC's pick, 2026-11-19).
- Five keepers, one pattern piece each, at the basin's own landmarks (BC, 2026-11-19: reuse landmarks):
  - Mira at the Leaning Tower: the Step. Wake the beacon on top. Climbing lasts half again as long.
  - Rana at the Nomad Camp market: the Sun. Buy a dye or a trail color.
  - Kip at the wind vanes: the Spiral. Have him weave one upgrade. Boost fills twice as fast.
  - Pell at the Oasis: the Water. Find his lost flute.
  - Sef at the Buried Ship: the Star. Climb the mast for him.
- Grandmother's carpet starts faded and wakes a little with each piece. A small woven mark for each piece shows on the carpet.
- The ending: bring all five home to Elder Suna. She asks which piece goes at the middle of your pattern. It becomes the carpet's middle and the carpet comes fully alive. Free roam after. You can change the middle later.
- Mira moved to the Leaning Tower's foot. Kip moved to the wind vanes, so Home Camp is just home now.
- The Journal, the Map, the postcard and the slot list show your pattern pieces.
- Two achievements changed name: The Drift (all five pieces) and Your Own Pattern (weave your design). They used to be the Scout's and Courier's sigils.

## Judgment calls

1. The five sites keep their landmark names (the Leaning Tower, the Nomad Camp and so on). The keepers call them by those names. Renaming them Red Steps, Glass Flats Market and the rest is a quick change.
2. Rana's Sun comes from buying anything at her stall. Kip's Spiral comes from any one upgrade.
3. The Step and the Spiral carry the old Scout and Courier gifts (longer climbing, faster boost refill). The other three pieces have no gift.
4. The ending happens when you talk to Elder Suna, at any time of day. The game has no dusk yet.
5. Tassel charms as the hidden keepsakes are not in this release. The six star stones stay as they are.
6. The two sigil achievements keep their saved ids with new names (G5.24). Anyone who earned them keeps them.
7. Mira's beacon thanks now ask you to come and see her at the tower foot.
