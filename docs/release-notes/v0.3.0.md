# Glasswind 0.3.0: The journey

2026-11-18. Kit 1.13.0. Installs over 0.2.0. Your save stays.

## What is new

- Four more requests, six in all. Pell at the Oasis and Sef at the Buried Ship join the basin. Ride the Old Road's three arches for Noor, carry seeds to Pell, find Pell's lost flute on the Dry Falls lip and climb the Buried Ship's mast for Sef.
- Sigils. Three Scout tokens or three Courier tokens and Elder Suna paints a sigil for your bike. The Scout's Sigil makes climbing last half again as long. The Courier's Sigil fills boost twice as fast.
- The ending. Tell Elder Suna who you will be. She shows the sigil on your bike, and the journey goes on. You can change it later.
- Six star stones on climbs, on the ship's deck, on a tall rock, on the rim and one in the air that only a glide reaches.
- The Map in the Menu. Every place you have found, and travel to it.
- Music: "Ancient Winds" by Kevin MacLeod (your pick).
- Sound: wind that rises with speed and height, the bike's hum, footsteps on sand and a soft pop when the glide bubble opens.
- A new low-poly hover bike and a softer, warmer sand light.
- Glasswind is on the Game Shelf.
- Fixed: 0.2.0 showed version 0.1.1 in its menus.

## Test checklist

1. Start a new game, find the bike parts and ride.
2. Do a request for each person. Check the Map fills in and travel works.
3. Finish three of one kind and see Elder Suna. Check the sigil shows on the bike.
4. Choose who you will be. Check the ending lines and that you can keep playing.
5. Glide from the Leaning Tower toward the Buried Ship and look for the star stone in the air.
6. Listen: music, wind, bike hum, footsteps, the glide pop. Check Settings sound levels.
